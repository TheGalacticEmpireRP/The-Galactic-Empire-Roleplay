04 // ARCHIVES

Frequently asked questions.

01How do I join The Galactic Empire?+

Join the Roblox group and Discord community using the buttons across the website. From there, follow the recruitment and verification instructions provided by command staff.

02Do I need experience to join?+

No. New members can learn through trainings, events, and guidance from division leadership. Activity, discipline, and willingness to improve matter most.

03How do I join a division?+

Division entry requirements vary. Some divisions may use applications, tryouts, academies, or rank requirements. Check the Discord for current recruitment information.

04Can I join more than one division?+

This depends on current Imperial policy and the requirements of each division. Always follow the latest rules published by High Command.

05What happens at events?+

Events can include trainings, patrols, raids, deployments, ceremonies, simulations, inspections, and large-scale roleplay operations.
