MEMBER GUIDE

Before you
deploy.

New to the Empire? Start here.

Ask the support team ↗
How do I join The Galactic Empire?+

Join our Roblox group and Discord community using the links on this website. Follow the recruitment and verification instructions provided by command staff.

Do I need roleplay experience?+

No. New members can learn through training, events, and guidance from division leadership. Activity, discipline, and a willingness to improve matter most.

How do I join a division?+

Entry requirements vary by division and may include applications, tryouts, academies, or rank requirements. Check Discord for current recruitment information.

Can I join more than one division?+

Follow the current division membership rules published by High Command. Division leadership can explain any restrictions or exceptions before you apply.

What happens at community events?+

Events can include training, patrols, raids, deployments, ceremonies, simulations, inspections, and larger roleplay operations.
